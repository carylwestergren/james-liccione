Welcome to the digital home of the Zora Neale Hurston Memorial. This historic monument stands proudly in Fort Pierce, Florida, where the legendary author, storyteller, and anthropologist spent her final years.

Designed and sculpted by Master Sculptor James Liccione, the memorial serves as a permanent tribute to her unparalleled contributions to American literature and African American folklore folklore folklore.

About Zora's Legacy

Zora Neale Hurston was a titan of the Harlem Renaissance. Best known for her masterful 1937 novel Their Eyes Were Watching God, her work celebrated the richness of Black culture and language. This page is dedicated to preserving her memory, her local history in St. Lucie County, and the beautiful public artwork that honors her spirit.
